🔊 Loudreader

Listen to any PDF as an audiobook for free.

Upload a PDF, EPUB, Markdown or paste text. Loudreader extracts and cleans the text, and then your browser's built-in TTS reads it with live word highlighting like an audiobook. You can optionally sign up to save the books you uploaded across sessions and devices, with automatic progress saving.
